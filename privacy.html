Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Screen Recorder: HD & Facecam ("the App"), published by The Zeus Labs, handles information.

1. Recordings Stay On Your Device

Every screen recording, along with any microphone, system audio, or facecam video captured, is saved directly to your device's own storage (in your phone's Movies folder, visible in your Gallery). The Zeus Labs does not collect, upload, view, or have any access to your recordings.

2. Permissions We Request and Why

  • Screen capture (MediaProjection) — required to record your screen; this is the App's core function.
  • Camera — only used if you enable the optional facecam overlay; the feed is composited directly into your local recording.
  • Microphone — only used if you choose an audio mode that includes mic or system audio.
  • Notifications — used to show the ongoing "recording in progress" status while you record.
  • Display over other apps — used to show the floating recording controls and facecam bubble while you record another app.
  • Internet and network state — used by the optional Go Live feature to send your broadcast to the streaming service you choose, and by Google services in the App (ads, purchases, update checks and crash reports). Your recordings themselves are never uploaded.

3. Live Streaming (Go Live)

If you use the optional Go Live feature, your screen and the audio you selected are sent directly from your device to the streaming service you choose — YouTube, Facebook, Twitch, or any custom RTMP server whose address you enter. The Zeus Labs operates no servers of its own, and your broadcast never passes through us: we cannot see, record, or store any part of it.

Once your broadcast reaches that service, it is governed entirely by that service's own terms and privacy policy, not by this one. Who can watch it, how long it is kept, and what is done with it are decided there. Please read the policy of whichever platform you stream to.

Your stream key is stored only in the App's private storage on your device, so you do not have to retype it. It is never sent anywhere except to the streaming server you chose, and it is never written to logs or shown on screen. Anyone holding your stream key can broadcast to your channel, so treat it as a password. Clearing the field, or clearing the App's data, removes it from your device.

4. Advertising and Your Consent

The free version of the App uses Google AdMob to show banner ads and a rewarded ad, shown only when you choose to unlock facecam or Go Live. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, app interactions and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads, governed by Google's own privacy policy. We do not separately collect or sell this data ourselves.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time from "Ad privacy choices" on the About screen, and reset your advertising ID in your Android settings. Users who buy Pro see no ads.

5. In-App Purchase (Pro)

Pro is an optional one-time purchase that removes ads and the rewarded-ad step before facecam and Go Live. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never receive your name, card or billing details. The App asks Google Play whether your account owns Pro, so it is restored automatically after a reinstall, and keeps a simple "Pro unlocked" flag on your device.

6. Crash Reports

To find and fix crashes, the App uses Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service. If the App crashes, it sends a crash report: the technical stack trace, the App version, the device model, Android version and similar diagnostic details, plus a random installation identifier. Crash reports contain no name, email, recordings, broadcast content or stream key, and we do not attach any identifier of yours to them. They are used only to fix problems in the App and are processed by Google under the Firebase terms.

7. Ratings and Updates

The App may show Google Play's in-app review prompt after it has worked for you, and may check Google Play for a newer version and offer to update. Any rating you leave and any update you install are handled by Google Play; the App does not see your rating.

8. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, contact list, precise location, or the contents of your recordings or broadcasts. We do not sell personal data to anyone. The App has no account, no sign-in, and no backend service that your content passes through.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.
