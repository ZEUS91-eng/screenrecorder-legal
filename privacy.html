Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Screen Recorder: HD & Facecam ("the App"), published by The Zeus Labs, handles information.

1. Recordings Stay On Your Device

Every screen recording, along with any microphone, system audio, or facecam video captured, is saved directly to your device's own storage (in your phone's Movies folder, visible in your Gallery). The Zeus Labs does not collect, upload, view, or have any access to your recordings.

2. Permissions We Request and Why

  • Screen capture (MediaProjection) — required to record your screen; this is the App's core function.
  • Camera — only used if you enable the optional facecam overlay; the feed is composited directly into your local recording.
  • Microphone — only used if you choose an audio mode that includes mic or system audio.
  • Notifications — used to show the ongoing "recording in progress" status while you record.
  • Display over other apps — used to show the floating recording controls and facecam bubble while you record another app.
  • Internet and network state — used only by the optional Go Live feature, to send your broadcast to the streaming service you choose. Recording alone never uses the network.

3. Live Streaming (Go Live)

If you use the optional Go Live feature, your screen and the audio you selected are sent directly from your device to the streaming service you choose — YouTube, Facebook, Twitch, or any custom RTMP server whose address you enter. The Zeus Labs operates no servers of its own, and your broadcast never passes through us: we cannot see, record, or store any part of it.

Once your broadcast reaches that service, it is governed entirely by that service's own terms and privacy policy, not by this one. Who can watch it, how long it is kept, and what is done with it are decided there. Please read the policy of whichever platform you stream to.

Your stream key is stored only in the App's private storage on your device, so you do not have to retype it. It is never sent anywhere except to the streaming server you chose, and it is never written to logs or shown on screen. Anyone holding your stream key can broadcast to your channel, so treat it as a password. Clearing the field, or clearing the App's data, removes it from your device.

4. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads (a rewarded ad, shown only when you choose to unlock facecam recording, plus banner ads). AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and general usage data to serve and measure ads, governed by Google's own privacy policy. We do not separately collect or sell this data ourselves.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, contact list, location, or the contents of your recordings or broadcasts. We do not sell personal data to anyone. The App has no account, no sign-in, and no backend service that your content passes through.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to contact.zeuslabs@gmail.com.
